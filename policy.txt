CUSTOMER SUPPORT COMPANY POLICY

RETURN POLICY

Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be unused and in their original packaging.

Some products may not be eligible for return because of hygiene,
customization, or other product-specific restrictions.


REFUND POLICY

Refunds are normally processed after the returned product is
received and inspected.

The refund may take 5 to 7 business days to appear in the
customer's bank account.


CANCELLATION POLICY

Customers can request cancellation before the order is shipped.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may not be possible.


SHIPPING POLICY

Standard shipping normally takes 3 to 7 business days.

Customers can ask the support assistant for their order status
by providing their order ID.


DELIVERY POLICY

Estimated delivery dates are estimates and may change because
of weather, transportation problems, holidays, or other delays.


CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Customers should provide their order ID when asking about a
specific order.

The support assistant must not invent order information.

If an order cannot be found, the customer should be asked to
verify the order ID.


PRIVACY POLICY

Customers should not provide passwords, credit card numbers,
bank account passwords, or other sensitive financial information
to the customer support assistant.
